How the numbers are worked out
  • Volume is the notional of every fill (size × price), maker and taker, across your wallets and sub-accounts.
  • Open interest is the current notional of your open positions at the exchange's mark price.
  • Hold time runs from when a position opens from flat to when it's fully closed. Adding or trimming doesn't restart the clock; a flip closes one position and opens the next. Bulk's own closed-position records are used where available.
  • Average open interest is your OI averaged over the range, time-weighted and valued at your fill prices.
  • Hedges group positions in the same asset across exchanges (NVDA-USD on Arcus and xyz:NVDA on Entropy count as NVDA, and an index perp priced like its ETF, such as mkts:US500 at SPY's price, joins SPY). Distance is how far the price has to move against a leg to reach its liquidation price. Bulk and Hyperliquid report liquidation prices; Arcus doesn't, so its price is estimated from the market's maintenance margin (marked ≈).
  • Entropy runs on Hyperliquid. Trades you place on entropy.io count on any market (Entropy tags its orders); trades from other apps on the same Hyperliquid account are left out.
  • Ranges are rolling. History is kept with your dashboard on the tracker's server, so it survives the exchanges' own history limits.

Arcus points name

Arcus only shows your own points when you're logged in, but its public leaderboard lists everyone's points under a points name. Enter yours, exactly as it appears on app.arcus.xyz/rewards (usually an adjective and an animal, like Rustic Owl), and the tracker loads your points, rank and tier.

Bulk AURA

Read from Bulk's AURA page for your Bulk wallet: total, pre-deposit AURA, weekly trading AURA, leagues and the next reveal. Bulk's site only answers web browsers, so after each Saturday reveal use the one-click Send AURA to tracker bookmark from the Points page.

Entropy points

Entropy hasn't launched points yet. Your Entropy trading is already being recorded, and you can log a total on the Points page as soon as Entropy shows one.
